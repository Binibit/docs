## What is the Bini App

The Bini App is a Telegram mini-app that gamifies entry into the Binibit ecosystem. Users mine **Crystals (CRS)**, level up via **lessons**, and bridge into BaiDEX once they hit the right gates.

| | |
|---|---|
| Levels | 30 (Seed → Genesis) |
| Lessons | 30 lessons (upgrade cards) |
| Mining | Background, claim every 4 hours |
| Bridges | A: Crystals → USBI · B: BINI off → BINI native |

## What will be documented

- Onboarding & Welcome Grant
- 30 Lessons (upgrade cards) — question, answer, facts, infographic
- Claim & mining mechanic (4-hour window, base 10K CRS/hr)
- Levels & progression
- USBI bridge (Bridge A, Level 5 gate)
- BINI bridge (Bridge B, Level 7 gate)
- Referrals (3-level CRS sharing)
- Franchise statuses
- Lottery (BINI sink)
